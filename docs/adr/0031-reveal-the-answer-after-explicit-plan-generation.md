# Reveal the answer after explicit plan generation

Keep Morning Review and durable provider history first on initial entry,
because unresolved prior work may need a human decision.

After an explicit recommendation request succeeds, scroll to the new plan and
put ranked candidates before schedule, host, quota, route, and methodology
detail. Within the plan, present:

1. ranked answer;
2. frozen full-night schedule and approval;
3. safety and capacity evidence;
4. exclusions and method.

Do not remove, collapse, or weaken provenance to make the answer prominent.
Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` when revealing the result.

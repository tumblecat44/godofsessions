# Selected route limitations are candidate risks

Copy limitations from the exact selected execution route into the recommended
candidate's risk list.

The detailed route inventory is supporting evidence, but answer-first
information architecture means a material limitation cannot exist only below
the schedule. Candidate risks are part of the approval decision and must carry
the same constraint.

Do not copy every generic guardrail into the candidate. Guardrails describe
how the adapter enforces safety; limitations describe capability or capacity
trade-offs the operator may use to reject the recommendation.

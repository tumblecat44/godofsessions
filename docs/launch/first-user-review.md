# First-user conversion review

## Verdict

The launch candidate now presents one ownable idea in the first viewport:
**Stop being the queue.** It immediately resolves the idea into the practical
promise **Every session. One clear next move**, then proves the product with a
22-second cut of the real English app.

The landing and product proof are ready for a private alpha. A frictionless
public Mac download is still blocked by Apple notarization, hosted-download
verification, and a clean-machine install pass—not by another missing product
feature.

## P0/P1 changes applied

- Rebuilt the landing around the ownable **night watch** thesis rather than a
  generic SaaS section stack.
- Replaced the launch proof with current English product captures; the longer
  brand film remains untouched.
- Added four chapter controls that seek to the exact product decisions and
  expose the active chapter with `aria-pressed`.
- Completed product-owned English strings across the shell, Control Board,
  Session Inbox, Settings, Overnight, bundled evidence, and preview
  conversation excerpts. User/provider-owned live titles remain verbatim.
- Added visible model-setting outcomes: saving, success, provider fallback,
  unsupported selection, and response-time lock explanations.
- Replaced the old Korean-product social image with a 1200×630 English landing
  preview.
- Converted the below-fold Morrow image from a 1.6 MB eager PNG to a 63 KB
  lazy-loaded WebP.
- Kept the support matrix explicit about observation, capacity, direct
  execution, and unsupported write paths.

## Visual and interaction checks

- Desktop checked in the connected browser at 1280×720.
- Mobile checked with an actual 390×844 viewport override.
- Both viewport checks reported no horizontal overflow.
- English and Korean hero states were visually captured after the redesign;
  the shorter Korean headline no longer forces a third oversized line.
- The proof is readable on mobile, stays edge-to-edge, and retains the
  `REAL APP UI · DEMO DATA` boundary.
- The third chapter seeks to 11.9 seconds, starts playback, and moves
  `aria-pressed` to the selected control.
- The proof reports 22.0 seconds, H.264 1920×1080 at 30 fps, 4.0 MB, no audio
  stream, and fast-start encoding.
- English/Korean switching updates the asynchronous DMG state; English was
  restored after testing.
- The local download HEAD returns the DMG rather than the HTML fallback.
- Reduced-motion behavior disables automatic looping and leaves normal video
  controls available.

## Remaining conversion risk

1. The unnotarized download still creates a trust-breaking Gatekeeper detour.
2. `sessions.vibejason.com` has not been connected to hosting, so the final
   HTTPS download path and social metadata have not been verified in public.
3. A clean Apple Silicon account has not completed download → Applications →
   onboarding → provider connection → first question → quit/reopen with
   quarantine preserved.

These are release-quality blockers. None is a reason to add another
orchestration feature.

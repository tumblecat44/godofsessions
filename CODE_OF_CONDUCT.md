# Code of Conduct

We want God of Sessions to be a constructive project for people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

Participants must:

- be respectful and assume good faith while discussing technical decisions;
- focus criticism on code, behavior, and evidence rather than people;
- welcome questions, corrections, and smaller contributions;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats, and unwanted contact;
- protect private data shared in issues, reviews, and development artifacts.

Unacceptable behavior may be removed from project spaces. Report conduct
concerns privately through the maintainer contact method in the repository
profile rather than escalating them in a public issue.
